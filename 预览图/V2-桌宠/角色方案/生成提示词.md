# 角色概念图生成记录

生成方式：内置 image_gen 图像工具。三张均为新生成的角色概念图，未替换运行中的桌宠。原始生成图保留，项目内保存副本。

## A · A-薄荷旅人

```text
Use case: stylized-concept.
Asset type: local character design review sheet for PayBeat, a tiny desktop walking companion.
Create a polished pixel-art character concept sheet, landscape 4:3, warm pale ivory background. Original chibi anime-inspired human sprite, facing right in three-quarter side view, designed to remain legible at 56 pixels tall. True low-resolution pixel art: visible square pixel clusters, hard stepped silhouettes, restrained 12–18-color palette, no vector smoothing, no gradients, no painted texture, no 3D. Hair and expressive anime eyes should distinguish the character; friendly everyday companion, fully clothed, not a fighter.
Layout: generous whitespace. Top half: one enlarged walking character beside a proper round pixel gold coin. Coin diameter is exactly HALF the full character height, not a tiny token. Coin has a round stepped silhouette, dark gold thickness on the right, bright raised rim, lighter inset face and a small embossed diamond. Full figure visible, not cropped.
Bottom half: a clean horizontal sequence of three equally spaced smaller frames using the SAME character at IDENTICAL scale: (1) walking right toward a coin positioned ahead at mouth height; (2) the coin reaches the character's open mouth and is partly swallowed with a tiny forward lean; (3) coin fully gone, happy closed-eye smile with two tiny gold sparkles. This must show physically approaching and eating the coin, not a coin teleporting overhead. Align all feet to one baseline. Tiny pixel ground marks only, no elaborate scenery. A soft arrow between frames is okay. No UI panels, no extra characters, no text other than the specified option label at top left. This is a design concept, not a finished production spritesheet.
Subject direction: Option A / MINT. Short fluffy mint-teal hair, side-swept fringe, warm amber anime eye, small cream scarf with a short trailing end, navy short jacket over a cream top, dark shorts with leggings, tiny brown ankle boots, one understated gold hair clip. Calm, curious and companionable. Strong clean silhouette with minimal accessories.
Text (verbatim): "A / MINT".
```
